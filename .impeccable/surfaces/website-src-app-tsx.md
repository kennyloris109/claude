# Surface: Ocean Marine Services home page

Scope: single-page marketing site, Persuade mode. Primary action: request a quotation (vessel name, IMO, port, ETA, items) by email.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a Karachi port call: a vessel arrives, sends its requisition, and gets supplied alongside. Refuses the marine-supplier template of stock-ship hero, icon-card service grid and invented stats.

OWN-WORLD: Chart paper #F2F5F3, harbour ink #0E2A3B, Q-flag yellow #F4C20D, ICS flag red #C8102E and blue #1D4F9C. Big Shoulders Stencil display (crate stencil), Barlow Condensed headings, Barlow body. Real Karachi Harbour photography and the 1918 Admiralty chart of Karachi Harbour. 2px ink manifest rules, 3px corners, signal-flag hoist as the brand mark.

STORY: A ship agent learns in one viewport that this is a chandler at Karachi Port and Port Qasim, sees the full stores range and the airline arm, trusts the port-specific process, and sends a complete requisition.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed slider of real Karachi Harbour photos with slow push-in and clip-path wipe transitions. Bottom-left: stencil wordmark (max 6rem), tagline, "Request a quotation" (yellow) and "See services". Right: O-M-S signal flags hoisting on load. Bottom rail: slide place name, coordinates, per-slide progress bars, pause control.

FORM: Harbour port-call narrative. Signature interaction: sticky Admiralty chart where scroll moves a vessel from Examination Anchorage up Manora Channel to the Kiamari wharves while the four supply steps advance. Services as a draggable photo slider; categories as photo tabs. Seed key: none. Disclosed deviation: concept-seed was not run for FORM. The client pinned the world ("the color fonts are right") and asked for specific content ("make sure it has some professional sliders and everything", "Build the website using: React, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui"); FORM was chosen directly from that brief without a dealt roll. Only OWN-WORLD was pinned by the client; the FORM choice is the builder's, reviewable by the client.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
