# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + TypeScript (Vite), Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui (Radix base), Motion for animation, Embla carousel. Chosen by the client. Static build, no backend.

## Users

Ship agents, masters, ship managers and procurement teams for vessels calling at Karachi Port and Port Qasim, often reading on a phone during a port call. Secondary: airline catering and operations staff sourcing provisions for foreign-going flights at Karachi.

## Product Purpose

Ocean Marine Services is a Pakistan-based ship chandler and marine supplier. The site explains what they supply and gets a visitor to send a quotation request containing vessel name, IMO number, port, ETA and required items.

## Positioning

A port-focused chandler built around Karachi Port and Port Qasim, covering provisions through technical stores as one supplier, with fast quotation and delivery coordination, plus airline provisions and stores.

## Operating Context

Quotation requests arrive by email (oceanmarineservices.pk@gmail.com). Vessels work to strict schedules; deliveries go alongside or by launch. Supplies are handled under customs, port, airport, airline, environmental and food-safety requirements.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services: ship chandling (provisions, deck, engine, cabin stores, galley equipment, bonded stores where applicable), marine equipment supply, lubricants and technical consumables, port and vessel support, tank cleaning support, waste management support (via authorized providers), airline provisions and stores.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Ocean Marine Services". Tagline "Reliable Supply. Professional Service. Portside Support." Closing line "Your Reliable Partner for Marine & Aviation Supply". Client approved the existing palette (harbour ink, chart paper, Q-flag signal yellow) and fonts (Big Shoulders Stencil, Barlow Condensed, Barlow).

## Evidence on Hand

Client-supplied copy only. Email is the only contact channel for now (client confirmed). No phone, address, logo, client names, certifications, statistics or testimonials exist: never invent them.

## Product Principles

- Get a complete quotation request in as few steps as possible.
- Show the ports and the port call, not generic "logistics".
- Every claim comes from the client's own copy.
- Readable on a phone on deck in daylight.
