Every AI image generator can now make a decent picture from a prompt. Where they differ is **taste**, **text** and **what you're allowed to do with the result**. These three lead on one each.

## How we compared them

We compared the jobs people actually buy image tools for: editorial and hero images, social graphics with headlines, product mockups, consistent characters or styles across a series, and editing existing photos. We also weighed free-plan access, API availability and commercial-use terms.

## Midjourney: best for looks

Midjourney's output still has the most *art direction* of any generator: lighting, composition and texture look deliberate rather than generic. It's the tool designers reach for when the image itself is the point.

- **Style references and moodboards** keep a visual identity consistent across dozens of images, which suits a blog or brand.
- **The web editor** handles inpainting, outpainting and retexturing without Discord.
- **Video** can animate your still images into short clips.

**Trade-offs:** There's no free plan and no public API, so it can't be automated into a publishing pipeline the way the other two can.

## Ideogram: best for text in images

Ideogram's headline feature is that **the words come out right**. Ask for a poster that says "Summer Sale: 40% off" and you get those exact words, legibly set. Few generators manage that reliably.

- Ideal for **YouTube thumbnails, Pinterest pins, social posts, event posters and logo exploration**.
- **Magic Prompt** expands a short idea into a detailed prompt.
- **An API** lets you generate graphics automatically.

**Trade-offs:** Its photographic and artistic output is very good but a step behind Midjourney's best.

## Adobe Firefly: best for commercial work

Firefly matters less as a standalone app than for **where it lives**: inside Photoshop (Generative Fill, Generative Expand), Illustrator and Adobe Express. Adobe also designed it for commercial use, which matters to agencies and brands.

- **Generative Fill** in Photoshop is still one of the most useful AI image features for real editing work.
- **Partner models** from other vendors are available inside the Firefly app on some plans, so you're not limited to Adobe's own model.
- **Enterprise IP indemnity** is available for businesses that need it.

**Trade-offs:** Pure prompt-to-image results can look more "stock photo" than Midjourney's, and the credit system across Adobe plans takes some reading.

## Quick decision guide

| You need… | Use |
| --- | --- |
| A stunning hero image or concept art | Midjourney |
| A graphic with a headline, price or logo | Ideogram |
| To edit or extend a real photo | Adobe Firefly (in Photoshop) |
| Images generated automatically by code | Ideogram or Firefly (both have APIs) |
| Client work with legal review | Adobe Firefly |

## The bottom line

[Midjourney](/tools/midjourney/) for beauty, [Ideogram](/tools/ideogram/) for words and [Adobe Firefly](/tools/adobe-firefly/) for commercial safety and Photoshop. If you only need occasional images, the generator built into [ChatGPT or Gemini](/compare/chatgpt-vs-claude-vs-gemini/) may be enough without another subscription.
