AI meeting notetakers join your calls, transcribe them and send a summary with action items when the meeting ends. Fathom, Fireflies and Otter all do this well, so the choice depends on how generous the free plan is, which tools the notes need to reach, and whether your meetings happen on video or in person.

## How we compared them

We looked at what people rely on these tools for: accurate transcripts with speaker names, useful summaries and action items, searching past meetings, and sending notes to the tools a team already uses. We gave free-plan limits extra weight because most people start on a free plan.

## Fathom: the best free plan

Fathom's free plan is the one most people can keep using indefinitely, because it includes unlimited recordings and transcripts. Only the advanced AI summaries are capped.

Its summaries are clean, and you can clip a key moment into a highlight with one click. Paid plans sync notes to a CRM for sales teams, and setup takes a few minutes.

Its team analytics and integrations are lighter than Fireflies', and it's built for video calls, so it's a poor fit for recording in person.

## Fireflies: best for teams and CRMs

Fireflies is built to send meeting notes wherever a team works. Summaries, action items and call notes go automatically into CRMs, project tools, Slack and hundreds of other apps through its integrations and Zapier.

Its conversation analytics show talk time, topics and sentiment across a team's calls. AskFred, its AI chat, answers questions about your whole meeting history, and it supports more meeting apps than the other two.

The free plan caps storage and AI credits, so active teams usually need a paid plan.

## Otter: best for live and in-person transcription

Otter has been around the longest, and it's still the best choice when there's no video call: lectures, interviews, focus groups and conference sessions.

Live transcription shows captions as people speak, which also helps with accessibility. The mobile app works well as a recorder for in-person conversations, and Otter AI Chat can answer questions about a meeting while it's still going.

The free plan caps monthly minutes and the length of each conversation, so regular users hit the limits fast.

## How to set up a notetaker without annoying everyone

Most complaints about AI notetakers are about bots joining meetings uninvited, more than about transcript quality. A few settings prevent that:

1. Set the bot to join only meetings you host, or only meetings with outside guests, instead of every event on your calendar.
2. Turn on the recording notice or chat message so everyone knows the call is being transcribed. In some places the law requires consent from everyone on the call.
3. Check where summaries go. Sending every summary to every attendee is handy inside a company but can leak notes to clients, so review the sharing settings before your first external call.
4. Decide how long recordings are kept, and delete what you don't need. Your security team will want to know.
5. Use the business plan for company data. Business tiers add admin controls, single sign-on and data-handling commitments that free plans don't have.
