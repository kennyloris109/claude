AI meeting notetakers join your calls, transcribe them and send a summary with action items when the meeting ends. Fathom, Fireflies and Otter all do the basics well, so the choice comes down to **free-plan limits**, **integrations** and **where your meetings happen**.

## How we compared them

We looked at what people rely on these tools for: accurate transcripts with speaker names, useful summaries and action items, searching past meetings, and sending notes to the tools a team already uses. Free-plan limits get extra weight because most people start there.

## Fathom: the best free plan

Fathom's free plan is the one most people can use indefinitely: **unlimited recordings and transcripts**. Advanced AI summaries are capped, but the core job is free.

- **Clean summaries** and one-click highlight clips of key moments.
- **CRM sync** on paid plans for sales teams.
- **A low-friction setup** that takes minutes.

**Trade-off:** Its team analytics and integrations are lighter than Fireflies', and it's built for video calls rather than in-person recording.

## Fireflies: best for teams and CRMs

Fireflies treats meetings as **data to route**. Summaries, action items and call notes flow automatically into CRMs, project tools, Slack and hundreds of apps through integrations and Zapier.

- **Conversation intelligence** shows talk time, topics and sentiment across a team's calls.
- **AskFred**, its AI chat, answers questions across your whole meeting history.
- **The widest platform support** covers more meeting apps than the other two.

**Trade-off:** The free plan caps storage and AI credits, so active teams usually need a paid plan.

## Otter: best for live and in-person transcription

Otter is the veteran, and it remains the best choice when **there's no video call**: lectures, interviews, focus groups, conference sessions.

- **Live transcription** shows captions in real time, which also helps accessibility.
- **The mobile app** is a strong recorder for in-person conversations.
- **Otter AI Chat** answers questions about a meeting while it's still going.

**Trade-off:** The free plan caps monthly minutes and conversation length, so regular users hit limits fast.

## Quick decision guide

| Your situation | Choose |

| Manager who wants to search months of past meetings | Fireflies |
| You need live captions during the meeting | Otter |

## How to set up a notetaker without annoying everyone

Most complaints about AI notetakers aren't about transcript quality. They're about bots turning up uninvited. A few settings avoid that:

1. **Limit auto-join.** Set the bot to join only meetings you host, or only meetings with external guests, instead of every event on your calendar.
2. **Announce it.** Turn on the recording notice or chat message so participants know the call is being transcribed. In some places, consent from everyone on the call is a legal requirement.
3. **Check where summaries go.** Auto-sharing summaries to every attendee is convenient internally but can leak notes to clients. Review the sharing defaults before your first external call.
4. **Set a retention policy.** Decide how long recordings are kept, and delete what you don't need. Your security team will ask.
5. **Use the business plan for company data.** Business tiers add admin controls, SSO and data-handling commitments that free plans don't include.
